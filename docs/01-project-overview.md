# 01 – Project overview

## Product vision

Provide one easy-to-use workspace to manage a travel customer's journey from enquiry through quotation, package confirmation, operations, collection, supplier settlement, trip completion and follow-up.

## Business profile

- Tour operator / travel agency.
- Direct-to-customer (B2C).
- Initial team size: 1–10 employees.
- Cloud-accessible.
- Responsive browser application, installable as a PWA.
- INR is the reporting/base currency; selected foreign currencies are supported.

## Travel categories

International holidays, domestic holidays, group tours, customized packages, corporate travel, honeymoon packages, hotel-only bookings, visa services and cruises.

## Product principles

1. **Package-centred:** every operational and financial record must be traceable to a Package ID.
2. **Simple operations:** short forms, sensible defaults, search, templates and quick actions.
3. **No duplicate data entry:** select existing customers, guests and suppliers wherever possible.
4. **Operational/financial separation:** a booking is a service record; a supplier bill is a liability record.
5. **Historical accuracy:** preserve transaction currency and exchange rate used at the time.
6. **Safe financial records:** no silent deletion or rewriting of issued invoices and posted transactions.
7. **Mobile-friendly:** key tasks must work on a phone-sized screen.

## Version 1 scope

Customer CRM, enquiries, follow-ups, quotations, packages, guests, flight/hotel/tour/transfer/visa/cruise bookings, suppliers, package-level customer receipts and supplier bills/payments, INR and foreign-currency recording, configurable GST invoice support, itinerary builder, document storage, WhatsApp/email/SMS notifications, dashboard, reports, employee permissions, cloud deployment and backups.

## Explicit exclusions

Full accounting ledger, payroll, inventory, public booking engine, customer portal, supplier portal, direct GDS/airline reservations, automated hotel reservations, multi-branch operations, native Android/iOS apps and advanced AI automation.

## Success measures

- A staff member can find all package information from one Package ID.
- A package can contain multiple bookings and multiple suppliers.
- Customer balances and supplier balances reconcile to allocated transactions.
- The team can produce a quotation, itinerary, invoice, receipt and service voucher.
- The system reduces duplicate entry and missed follow-ups.
- Core screens work on desktop and mobile browsers.
