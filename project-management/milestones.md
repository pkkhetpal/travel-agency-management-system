# Development milestones

| Milestone | Target window | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|
| M0 Discovery/sign-off | Week 1 | SRS, wireframes, ERD, integration assumptions approved |
| M1 Platform foundation | Week 2 | Login, roles, company settings and master data work |
| M2 Sales workflow | Week 3 | Customer → enquiry → quotation → package works |
| M3 Travel operations | Weeks 4–5 | Multiple guests/bookings/suppliers and documents work |
| M4 Package finance | Week 6 | Receipts, bills, allocations, currency and tax tests pass |
| M5 Itinerary and messaging | Week 7 | Itinerary PDFs and channel test messages work |
| M6 Reports and readiness | Week 8 | Reports, responsive checks, security checks complete |
| M7 UAT and launch | Weeks 9–10 | Acceptance passed, production deployed, backup restored, team trained |

## Commercial milestone suggestion

For agency contracts, a possible payment split is:
- 10% after commencement and approved design.
- 20% after sales/package workflow.
- 20% after booking/guest/supplier workflow.
- 20% after finance/currency/GST workflow.
- 15% after itinerary/documents/notifications.
- 15% after UAT, deployment and handover.

This is a negotiation suggestion, not a project quote. Tie payments to demonstrable deliverables and acceptance evidence.
